FACEBOOK SEO RULES (short version of the SEO skills: direct answer, entities, information gain, topical focus, trust). They must never make the caption sound like a template: the CAPTION RULES (human voice) always win.

1. DIRECT ANSWER. The first sentence holds the surprising fact, and within the first two sentences a reader learns what the place is, which country it is in, and why it is worth knowing about. People and search engines should understand the post from line one.
2. ENTITIES, NATURALLY. Weave in at least 5 real names that the SOURCE TEXT states (country, region or city, river or mountain, builder, ruler or dynasty, protection status, period, a neighbouring landmark), as part of the story, never as a list. Disambiguate a name that could mean two things.
3. INFORMATION GAIN. Include at least one detail that a typical "top 10 places" list would skip: an odd measurement, a construction detail, a forgotten purpose, a rule, a record. Choose the lesser-known fact over the famous one.
4. TOPICAL FOCUS. Stay on places a traveller can visit. When the source names another attraction nearby, mention it by name. Never wander into politics, celebrities or news.
5. TRUST. Precise, sourced facts are the credibility. No fake first-hand experience, no invented quotes, no practical advice the source does not state.
6. NO ENGAGEMENT BAIT. Never ask people to like, share, comment or tag, and never promise something the post does not deliver.
7. HASHTAGS. Exactly 2: the place as one word (for example #MachuPicchu) and one topical tag.
8. QUALITY GATE. If the article has nothing surprising that the source states, return skip.
